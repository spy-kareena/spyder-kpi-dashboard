# Spyder KPI Dashboard 2v2 - User Quick Start

1. Open the dashboard link from IT.
2. Run the SAP KPI query (`queries/sap_b1_hana_kpi_current_dashboard_so_po_with_lagging.sql`).
3. Export the result to Excel (SAP saves it as an `.xls` file; upload it as-is).
4. Upload the file into the dashboard.
5. Use Order Health and Purchase Orders (left sidebar) to review risks, reschedules, OTD and notes. Click a KPI card to see its lines; **Add note** on a line sets an override, the cause (**Us** = Spyder, **Them** = the customer, **Auto** = from the status and SAP flags) or a note.
6. Optional: run `queries/sap_b1_hana_supply_demand_snapshot_v2.sql`, export it and load it with **Add file** (bottom of the sidebar) for Supply / Demand and Supply Mix.
7. Optional: add the "Orders At Risk - Clear to Build" workbook with **Add file** for Build Risk. It opens on the newest week; pick another under **Report week**.

**What does this number mean?** Rest the pointer on any card, chart bar, column heading, filter or button (or Tab to it, or press and hold on a phone) for a short tip with the full amounts. Rest on a section title the same way for what that section shows and what clicking does; **About these numbers** at the bottom of each page defines the key terms. First-visit hints close with ×; **Show hints again** (sidebar, Help) brings them back.

Order Health opens with a large **Leading / Lagging** switch at the top, each half with its headline number (at-risk value; on-time delivery over 3 months). **Leading** is what needs action now: At risk, the open order states, the top at-risk reasons, **When flagged** (weeks since each line was first at risk) and **Us vs Them** (Us = Spyder: production, quality, supply or planning; Them = the customer or carrier). **Lagging** is how deliveries went (on time, shipped this week, fill rate, reschedule hits, perfect orders, average days late), each with who caused the misses. Arrow keys switch tabs. Click a reason, Us / Them or a when-flagged bar to see only those lines; the chips under the filters (or **Reset**) remove those filters. Six small tiles under the At risk card show its lines and open value by override (Shipping Delay, QC Delay, R&D Hold, Supplier Delay, Supply Chain Delay, Wait on Prod): click one to list only those lines, click again to show all. A line's cause comes from its override (for example Hold = Them, Supplier Delay = Us) or its SAP dates (no commit date or past due with no reschedule = Us); a plain reschedule is "Not set" until someone picks Us or Them. **Top offenders** shows each name as $20,000 blocks; click a customer, warehouse or vendor to filter the page.

The **Warehouse scorecard** (Order Health) lists CM SFG warehouses and all other warehouses as two groups, each with a subtotal row. Click a row to show only that warehouse; click again to show all.

In Order Health’s Lines to act on, tick rows (or the header box for the whole list) to set an override or a cause (**Set cause**) on many lines at once, or delete them. Deleted lines are hidden on every page (for everyone, on the hosted site); restore them from **Data check**. A short confirmation appears after Apply, Delete, Restore and when you close a note. Click a column header to sort. On Purchase Orders, sort with **Sort lines** and use **Add note** on each line.

The search box under the logo (or Ctrl+K, Cmd+K on a Mac) finds any order line by number, customer, vendor, item, warehouse or FedEx tracking number, or by a reason such as "no commit date" or "caused by us".

**Shipping** (sidebar, Sales) shows where recent FedEx shipments are: a strip from label created to delivered, what is late against the promised date, exceptions, and what is held at customs (Cross-border). Click a shipment for its order lines and scans; click a card to list only those shipments. Tracking numbers come from SAP deliveries (fill **Tracking No.** on the delivery and use the updated KPI query); type others in **Add tracking #** with the sales order number. FedEx is checked every 4 hours; **Refresh now** checks right away (once every 10 minutes). Live status works on the hosted site only. Setup: docs/SHIPPING.md.

**Big picture** (the switch at the top right of Order Health, Build Risk and Supply Mix) shows a flow chart at the top of the page (on Order Health it is on until you switch it off); your browser remembers it. Thick bands carry most of the value. Order Health: pick open order value (warehouse to status to customer segment) or at-risk value (reason to Us / Them to customer). Build Risk: each CM's orders by clear to build, at risk and late, by month. Supply Mix: open demand by warehouse, item group and month; red bands are short of open purchase orders. Point at a box for its numbers (keyboard: Tab to the chart, arrow keys between boxes). Click a box to filter the page to it (one warehouse, customer or CM); click again to show everything. On a phone, swipe sideways inside the chart. Turn the switch off to hide it.

**Ask AI** opens a chat on the right. Ask things like "What is at risk right now?" or "Which vendors have the most late POs?". The first time, pick a provider in **Settings** and tick its box: Claude or ChatGPT (paid; they do not train on your data) or Groq or OpenRouter (free; they may keep what you send, so use them only for data you may share). It sends your question and the data needed to answer it to that provider. Setup, costs and privacy: docs/AI_ASSISTANT.md.

**Help > Connect an AI** in the sidebar shows how to use the same data from Claude Desktop, Claude Code or another MCP app; it opens before an export is loaded.

The page header shows the date the KPIs are calculated for (the export date). If the amber **Data check** pill appears, click it for the SAP rows with unreadable dates (for example a year typed as 0202 or 2626); fix those in SAP and re-export.

The dashboard remembers the last files loaded: anyone who opens the hosted site sees that report, on any computer, until someone loads a new export with **New file** (which replaces it for everyone). The sidebar says "Saved for everyone who signs in" once it is stored; "Saved in this browser only" means the site has no shared storage.

Notes, overrides, causes and deleted lines are shared too: on the hosted site everyone who signs in sees the same ones, on any computer. The first time you edit a note the dashboard asks for your name so others can see who made the last change: click your name if it is listed, or type it under **New name** (it is then listed for everyone). **Not now** skips it, and **Change name** in the sidebar changes it later. The sidebar shows "Notes shared · last edit Kareena · Oct 8". If two people change the same line, the later change wins. If the server cannot be reached, your changes stay in your browser and the dashboard sends them again by itself (or the next time you open it). If the sidebar says some changes were not saved (for example your sign-in expired), reload the page and sign in: they are sent then. "Notes saved in this browser only" means the site has no shared storage (for example an IIS copy). **Share snapshot** makes a file that includes all loaded rows and the notes on them.

Each upload on the hosted site also records which lines were at risk that week (one entry per week, last 52 weeks kept), so the dashboard can tell how long a line has been at risk.
